The Strike Room

One screen to run an ACE call. Move top to bottom. Do NOT open the demo until the number gate is green. End on a pinned next step, every time.

The 3 leaks that killed the last 3 calls. Do not repeat:
  • No pinned next step (ended on "give me a day" x3). Never end open.
  • ROI asked, never answered. Give the number from THEIR MRR + goal.
  • Price/affordability walked out alive. Handle it live, do not accept "I'll think about it".
0Before the call2 min
  • Open Walkthrough, Proof Room, Offer Page in tabs (buttons up top).
  • Have one clone reel ready to screen-share (Arfeen output = sharpest; Madhu for doctors).
  • Fathom recording ON.
  • Know their niche + one specific piece of their content to reference.
1Open + take the frame0-3 min

Acknowledge the win first (high-status people need the win named before the offer). Then earn permission to diagnose.

"Before I show you anything, is it okay if I ask a few questions to figure out where the actual bottleneck is? Otherwise I'd just be pitching you blind."
This line held 20 min on the first call. Use it verbatim. Whoever asks the questions controls the frame.
2Money gate + diagnosis3-18 min
GATE. Do not move to the demo until every box below has a number. The last 3 calls demoed before diagnosing and pitched the wrong thing. Ask, then STOP talking.
  • How many members/clients do you have right now?
  • What's your price / tiers?
  • So what's your MRR right now?
  • Where do members come from today, content, referrals, ads, the Skool network?
  • How many hours a week go into content? On top of everything else?
  • Where do you want to be in 6-12 months, members or revenue? (the GOAL)
  • What are you spending on marketing now?
  • What's the real bottleneck, in your words?

Capture before you proceed

Fill these live. The demo and the ROI math both use them.
3Name the wound18-25 min

Reflect their own numbers back. Add nothing new. Sell what they actually want (members/revenue), never "reach".

"So the work is good, you've done [X], and it's costing you [hours]. And it's bringing in [their result]. The work isn't the problem. It just isn't travelling / your reach isn't converting to members."
"You don't need a million followers. You need the right people who'd pay for [their offer] to actually find you."
Then STOP. Let it sit. Do not rescue the silence.
4Demo: show, do not tell25-33 min
Hard cap 8 min. Feature-monologues triggered feature-brain on every past call. Say the OUTCOME, show ONE thing, stop.
  • Open the Walkthrough. Show the clone beside the original. Ask: "Can you tell that isn't them?"
  • Show the funnel + dashboard = they see REVENUE, not views.
  • Say "cloning" LATE and once. Sell the whole chain (strategy + scripting + production + publishing). Cloning is ~10%.
What lands every time: the side-by-side clone, and "he doesn't spend on ads either". Lead with those.

5Offer + price33-38 min
  • Stack the value OUT LOUD first (build + strategy + scripting + clone + 10 reels + funnel).
  • Reveal the FOMO: founding batch of 10, 1-2 per niche.
  • State the price ONCE: "$2,000 founding, month 1 is the full build plus 10 reels."
Then SILENCE. Do not fill the gap. Do not offer a second option. Do not discount. Past calls self-negotiated before any objection and discounted air. On the Greg call the price landed then he kept talking through tiers and justification, no silence.
State it as ONE DOLLAR FIGURE, never a percentage: "$2,000 founding, $5,000 standard from September." Do NOT say "50% off", it invites math, and last time the math was wrong ($5k to $2k is 60%, not 50%).
Anchor before the number, in THEIR math: "One or two new members a month covers this. At your [MRR/price], what does that look like over a year?"
Fee is absorbed, clean $2,000, never a surcharge. Send the Offer Page to take payment (card or bank).
6Objection killersas they come

Tap any objection for the punch. Built from real calls (Shubhra, Akshay, Greg).

"Let me think about it / give me a day"

This is THE leak (3x). Do not accept it open.

"Totally fair. Genuinely, what's the part you want to think through, the money, or whether it'll actually work for you?" [then handle THAT live]
"Here's what I'll do. I'll hold your founding seat until [date/time]. Let's get 10 minutes then so you're not deciding in a vacuum, and I'll answer whatever's still open." [book it in the calendar NOW]
Price / "weighing my finances" / can't afford

Handle LIVE. Never let it walk (killed Shubhra + Greg). Give the ROI number.

"One or two new members a month covers the whole thing. You're at [members] at [price]. Fifty more over a year is [$]. This pays for itself and then some."
Reason-why for the rate: "It's a founding-partner exchange, not a discount, you give a case study, I give the founding price." Then payment options (card or bank), do not drop the number.
"The algorithm burned me / it just stopped showing my stuff" (the PTSD wound)

Greg's core wound, verbatim: "I got a little PTSD... Facebook just stopped showing my stuff." Validate the scar first, do NOT argue it away.

"That's real, and it's exactly why one-platform organic is a trap. You fed one algorithm by hand and it turned the tap off. ACE runs you daily across five platforms at once, so you're never at the mercy of one feed again. And it's owned organic that compounds, not rented ads that stop the second you stop paying."
Do NOT promise recovery speed or specific view numbers (no data to back it). Sell the de-risk: never one platform, never by hand again.
Cash-constrained: "it's a lot for me right now"

Greg: "baby on the way, haven't made money in three years... but I'm always willing to spend money to make money." Real constraint + real willingness. Give a lower-friction ENTRY, do not discount the price.

"Totally hear you. Two ways to make this easy: one, we start with a deposit to lock your founding seat and split the rest over the build month. Two, you're already spending $25/day on ads the algorithm eats, redirect even part of that into this and it's close to cost-neutral, except this one you OWN."
Offer a deposit / split before you ever cut the price. Redirect-the-ad-spend is the cleanest reframe for anyone already paying for reach.
"I've tried things before and they didn't work"

MOST dangerous objection, every expert has this scar. Do NOT defend, ASK.

"What did you try, and where exactly did it break?" [listen]
"That's the difference. A tool gives you a clip and you still do everything else. This is the whole chain, strategy, scripting, production, publishing, so the thing that failed you before, [their gap], is the exact thing we run."
"What's the ROI / the return?"

Asked twice on the Akshay call, never answered (metaphor dodge). NEVER dodge. Use their numbers.

"At [their price], you need [1-2] new members a month to break even. The engine runs daily across every platform. If it brings even [X] a month, that's [$] a year on a one-time [$2k] plus [retainer]. That's the ROI."
"I can just use HeyGen myself"

Do not argue. Ask the 4 questions.

  • Is your clone actually indistinguishable, or does it read as AI?
  • Are you cloning a talking head, or the whole business, frameworks and offers?
  • Does cloning alone solve it, or do you also need strategy, scripting, algorithm work?
  • Even with a perfect clone, will you still need someone editing and publishing per platform?
"Nobody wants a clip. They want the outcome the clip was supposed to deliver."
"Who owns the clone / IP?"
"It's your IP. I'm a blender to you." (Arfeen has a UAE company + agreement as precedent.) Worked cleanly before.
"My clients are premium, offers don't move them"

Dropped entirely on the Akshay call. Reframe the funnel.

"Then it's not a discount funnel, it's an assessment-and-consult path. The content builds trust and brings the right premium people to a real conversation, not a coupon."
"Is this ethical / authentic for me?"
"You approve every script, always, permanently. It stays in your voice and your values. The machine handles the mechanics, you keep the message and the mission."
For clinicians: approval is ALWAYS theirs, forever. Never say "Madhu doesn't approve anything" (contradiction flagged on the Akshay call).
"Can I take the clone elsewhere later?"
"Yes, after the engagement. It's yours, you'd only forgo the build cost. No lock-in on the asset itself."
7The close: pin a next steplast 7 min
NEVER end without ONE of these three:
  • Payment taken (send the Offer Page, card or bank).
  • A decision call BOOKED with a date + time (in the calendar before you hang up).
  • A deadline THEY name, with the next call already booked to hear it.

The temperature check:

"On a scale of 1 to 10, how ready do you feel to start?" [if not 10] "What would make it a 10?" [handle exactly that, then close again]

If they hesitate, ISOLATE then ASK immediately. Do NOT drift back into feature questions, that is exactly what lost the Greg deal (he isolated "is it just money?", Greg said yes, then Akassh answered 15 more minutes of Q&A instead of closing):

"Is it just the money, or is there something else holding you back?" [if just money] → go straight to the deposit / split + redirect-your-ad-spend close. Do not answer another feature question.

The assumptive close:

"Want to grab one of the founding seats now? I can send the link, card or bank, whichever's easier."
"I'll send you the details" is NOT a close. If they need to think, you book the follow-up on the spot. Do not leave the call on a message you have to chase.
