Refund & Cancellation Policy

GetCloned · Peepoye Network · Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

This policy covers GetCloned services and any subscription products offered under Peepoye Network, including GetVisible. It explains cancellations, refunds, and how to reach us.

2. Cancellation

You can cancel a subscription anytime by emailing hello@getcloned.in. Your access continues until the end of the current billing period. No further charges are made after cancellation.

3. Refunds

Subscription products offer a free trial period so you can test the service fully before you pay. Because of this, subscription fees are generally non-refundable once charged. If you believe a charge was made in error, write to us within 7 days of the charge and we will review and resolve it, including a full refund where the charge was wrong or the service was unusable.

4. Done-For-You Services

For custom done-for-you engagements, refund terms are set in the individual service agreement signed before work begins. Those terms apply to that engagement.

5. How To Reach Us

Email hello@getcloned.in with your account email. We reply within 2 business days.
