Shipping & Delivery

GetCloned · Peepoye Network · Last updated: July 2026

1. Digital Service

GetCloned and its products (including GetVisible) are fully digital services. Nothing physical is shipped. There are no shipping charges and no delivery addresses.

2. Delivery

Access to digital products is delivered online. Subscription products are available instantly inside the web app after signup or payment. Done-for-you content is delivered digitally per the timeline agreed in your service agreement.

3. Trouble Accessing

If you cannot access a product after signup or payment, email hello@getcloned.in and we will resolve it within 2 business days.
